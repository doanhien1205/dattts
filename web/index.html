    For developers

    One curl, one MP3.

    A simple JSON API: send text, get back the audio URL, subtitles and a timestamp for every sentence. For long texts, queue a job and poll its progress.

    POST /api/tts
    curl https://dattts.com/api/tts \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"text": "[Trúc Ly] Chào anh. [Thiện Minh] Chào em.",
       "speed": 1.0, "format": "mp3"}'

# → {"audio_url": "/api/files/….mp3", "srt_url": "…", "duration": 2.9,
#    "segments": [{"text": "Chào anh.", "start": 0.0, "end": 1.1}, …]}

    On Pro? Add X-API-Key: DATTTS-… to every request to use your plan’s allowance instead of the free per-IP quota. Check your plan and usage with GET /api/me. Error messages follow your Accept-Language header (English or Vietnamese).

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How are characters counted?

    Every letter, digit, punctuation mark and space counts as one character — accented letters like “ữ” included. Regenerating an identical take, or downloading it again as WAV or OGG, is free. Dictionary replacements count at their expanded length.

    Can I use the audio commercially?

    Yes. The voice model is released under the Apache-2.0 license, which allows commercial use of all 25 built-in voices. Use your files in ads, monetized videos, apps and products you sell. DatTTS uses these 25 preset voices only — there is no voice cloning and no uploading of your own or anyone else’s voice.

    Do I need to speak Vietnamese?

    No, but your text must be in Vietnamese — DatTTS doesn’t translate. Paste a translation from your team or translator and the studio reads it with the right tones. Numbers, dates, currency and units are expanded and read the Vietnamese way automatically.

    Which accents are available?

    Northern (Hanoi), Central and Southern (Saigon) accents, female and male, in three styles: natural, news and storytelling.

    How do I get two people talking in one file?

    Type the voice name in square brackets before each line, e.g. [Trúc Ly] Chào anh. [Thiện Minh] Chào em. The “Insert voice” menu in the studio does it for you.

    Who sells Pro and issues invoices?

    Polar (Polar Software Inc.) is our Merchant of Record. Polar takes the payment, charges sales tax or VAT according to your country, and emails your invoice and receipt. Your card statement may show Polar instead of DatTTS.

    How can I pay?

    Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Apple Pay and Google Pay, processed by Polar. Prices are in US dollars. In Vietnam? International Visa or Mastercard cards (debit cards included) work; domestic ATM cards that only run on the Napas network are not accepted.

    Where do I get my API key?

    Right after checkout, Polar emails your receipt with an “Access purchase” button. It opens Polar’s customer portal, where your API key (starting with DATTTS) is waiting — copy it, click “Enter API key” in the studio and paste it in. The “Manage plan” button in the studio opens the same portal. If a key leaks, rotate it there; the old key stops working within about 10 minutes.

    How do I cancel or get a refund?

    Cancel anytime in the Polar customer portal — you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for and won’t be charged again. First purchase not working out? Ask within 14 days (3 days on Weekly) and, if the characters you’ve used since the charge are under 10% of one period’s allowance, we refund you in full. See the refund policy.

    When do the allowances reset?

    All resets use Vietnam time (UTC+7). Free: daily at midnight. Pro Weekly: every Monday at 00:00. Pro Monthly and Yearly: on the 1st of each month. Unused characters don’t roll over.
