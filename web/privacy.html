Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Đọc bằng tiếng Việt: Chính sách bảo mật. This English version is the governing version.

This policy explains what data DatTTS (dattts.com), operated by [Legal name], collects and processes, why, and for how long. We collect only what the service needs to work, and we process personal data in line with Vietnam’s personal data protection law and, where it applies to you, the GDPR and similar laws.

In short: no account needed; your text is used only to generate speech; generated files are cached so you can replay them, and you can ask us to delete them anytime; payments and purchase emails are handled by Polar; we never sell data and never train models on your text.

1. Data we process

  • Text and options you submit (voice, speed, format, pronunciation dictionary): processed to generate audio and subtitles. The text is embedded in the result (subtitles and timing data) and cached together with the file.
  • Audio and subtitle files: cached so you can replay them and download other formats without using more characters. You can ask us to delete them anytime at support@dattts.com (include the file link). Anyone with a file link can open the file; links contain a hard-to-guess ID, but don’t share them if the content is sensitive.
  • IP address: used to count the free daily allowance, rate-limit API key checks and checkout sessions, and prevent abuse. We keep daily, hourly and per-minute counters and don’t link IP addresses to your identity.
  • API key: when you send a key, our server asks Polar to validate it and caches the result under a SHA-256 hash of the key, never the key itself. The cache entry holds the Polar license key ID, your Polar customer ID, your plan and the email address Polar has for you.
  • Usage records: for each license key we keep the number of characters used per day and per billing period, with the first and last time it was used and your Polar customer ID (not your text and not your email). We need these to apply allowances and to check refund requests.
  • Email address: you enter it on Polar’s checkout page. We receive it from Polar to send important notices about your plan and to support you; on our site it’s only ever shown partially masked. If you join the waitlist, we keep that email to tell you when Pro opens.
  • Billing events: Polar notifies us when a subscription is created, renewed, cancelled or revoked; we keep a compact log (the latest 500 events) to run the service and support customers.
  • Data in your browser: your last 10 takes, pronunciation dictionary, theme, language preference and API key are kept in your own browser’s local storage and are sent to our server only when a request needs them. Clear them with “Remove key” or by clearing site data.
  • Language: your browser sends an Accept-Language header so we can reply with error messages in English or Vietnamese. We don’t store it.

We never receive or store card numbers or bank details — those are handled by Polar and its payment partner (Stripe). The site uses no advertising cookies or third-party trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address when the fonts load.

2. Why we use it

  • To generate audio and subtitles and let you download the results again.
  • To apply the free allowance and paid plan allowances.
  • To validate API keys, manage plans, support customers and handle refund requests.
  • To keep the service secure, detect abuse and comply with the law.

3. Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your data. We share it only with:

  • Polar Software Inc. — our Merchant of Record: payments, tax, invoices, refunds, API key issuance and the customer portal. Polar processes payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Infrastructure providers: Modal Labs (servers, file storage and caching) and our domain and static hosting providers. They process data only on our behalf to run the service.
  • Competent authorities when the law requires it, or when needed to stop fraud, impersonation or other breaches of our Terms of Service.

Our servers and Polar’s may be located outside Vietnam (mainly in the United States). By using the service, you agree to this transfer for the purposes in section 2.

5. Your rights

You have the right to be informed, to access, correct and delete your personal data, to withdraw consent and to object to processing. Email support@dattts.com (include the email you used at checkout, or the link of the file you want deleted early). We reply within 7 business days. For payment data you can also contact Polar directly.

6. Security

Connections to DatTTS are encrypted with HTTPS; API keys are stored only as hashes; access to servers and admin data is limited to the operator. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident affects your data, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

7. Children

The service isn’t meant for children under 16 to use on their own. Anyone buying a paid plan must be old enough to enter into the transaction under the laws where they live.

8. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top and email paying customers about significant changes. Privacy questions: support@dattts.com.
