Refund Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Đọc bằng tiếng Việt: Chính sách hoàn tiền. This English version is the governing version.

We want you to pay only if DatTTS is genuinely useful to you. This policy covers the Pro Weekly, Pro Monthly and Pro Yearly plans bought through Polar.

Polar Software Inc. is our Merchant of Record. All refunds are issued by Polar to the payment method you used: you send the request to us, we approve it, and Polar processes the refund.

1. Full refund on your first purchase

You get a 100% refund of your first payment for a Pro plan if both of these are true:

  1. you ask within the refund window after the charge; and
  2. the characters your key has used from the charge until your request add up to less than 10% of one period’s allowance (the amounts below), according to our character counter. This counts all usage since the charge, even if it spans two calendar weeks or months.
PlanRefund windowUsage must be under
Pro Weekly3 days25,000 characters (10% of 250,000 for the first week)
Pro Monthly14 days100,000 characters (10% of 1,000,000 for the first month)
Pro Yearly14 days100,000 characters (10% of the first month’s 1,000,000 allowance)

We also always refund in full any duplicate charge, or any charge caused by a technical error on our side or Polar’s.

2. Everything else

  • Renewal charges, requests outside the refund window, or usage at or above 10% of the first period’s allowance: no refund. You can cancel anytime so you won’t be charged again, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If the service is down because of us for more than 72 hours in a row, we’ll consider a partial refund for the time affected.
  • Keys suspended for breaking our Terms of Service (fraud, impersonation, illegal content…): no refund.

3. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email support@dattts.com from the address you used at checkout, with your plan and order ID (or the invoice number from Polar’s email). A reason is optional but helps us improve.
  2. We look up the characters your key has used since the charge in our usage records and reply within 3 business days.
  3. Once approved, Polar refunds the card or wallet you paid with. It usually reaches your account within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

When we approve a refund, we ask Polar to cancel that subscription and revoke its API key at the same time; the key stops working on DatTTS within minutes. Refunds are made in USD; exchange-rate differences and currency conversion fees charged by your bank are outside our and Polar’s control.

4. Chargebacks

If something’s wrong with a charge, please contact us first — it’s almost always faster than a dispute through your bank. This policy doesn’t limit any consumer rights you have under the law where you live.
